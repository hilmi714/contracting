A-02

The comparison problem

The comparison problem

Why the lowest quote usually costs you the most.

You get quotes for the same empty unit and the numbers land far apart. Nothing on the page explains the gap. One quote leaves out make-up air because nobody had specified the kitchen equipment yet. Another assumes your landlord moves the sprinklers. Drawings show up as a separate purchase on the third. Nobody is lying to you. They answered different questions, and you are the one who finds out which one later, usually on the morning the walls come open.

  • What the number shows

    A total and a start date.

  • What it hides

    Which trades are excluded and who actually carries the permit.

A complete quote names the permit holder and lists what it left out. If it cannot do that, it is a placeholder, not a price.

The chain

How we hold a date

One contract holds the drawings and the inspection.

Most builds hand you off between companies. The designer finishes, the permit waits in a queue, the trades bid against each other, and the inspection becomes somebody else's problem. Every handoff is a place where a date slips and nobody owns it. Ours does not. We keep all of it inside one contract, so there is one schedule and one name against it.

Five stages, in order: Draw, Submit, Lock, Build, Inspect.

  1. Draw

    Our architects and engineers turn your plan into drawings the City will accept.

  2. Submit

    We prepare the permit submission and file it with the City of Calgary.

  3. Lock

    We sign the scope and the trade schedule before demolition starts.

  4. Build

    Our crews and trades work the same schedule, and we manage it on site every day.

  5. Inspect

    We coordinate each inspection and stay on it through to occupancy.

Each stage belongs to the same contract. Nothing gets handed to a company you have never met.

A-08

Questions

Questions

The questions we get before the first site visit.

  • How long will my build-out take?

    It depends on your scope and on City of Calgary review times, and we will not put a number on it before we have walked the unit and read your lease. What we can show you is how the schedule is built and where the long-lead items sit.

  • Who deals with the City?

    We do. We prepare the submission, file it, answer the reviewer's questions and book every inspection through to occupancy. You sign where a signature is legally required and nothing else.

  • What is actually in the quote?

    A line-item scope that names the permit holder and lists every exclusion in writing. If something is not included, you read it before you sign, not after the walls open.

  • Can you work around other tenants?

    Yes. We plan noisy work around building hours and protect the common areas, because your landlord's other tenants are the ones who complain first.

  • Are you insured?

    Fully insured, and we send the certificate of insurance to your landlord or property manager on request. Ask for it before you sign anything, with us or with anyone else.
