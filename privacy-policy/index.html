01Who we are

Stallion Contracting is a commercial general contractor based at 7500 Macleod Trl SE, Suite 202, Calgary, Alberta T2H 0L9. This policy explains what personal information we collect through stallioncontracting.ca, how we use it, and the choices you have.

We handle personal information in line with Alberta’s Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA).

02Information you give us

When you request a quote or site visit through our form, we collect the answers you choose (such as project type, space size and timeline), your name, phone number, and, if you provide them, your email address and any message you write.

If you call or email us, we keep the details you share so we can respond and follow up on your project.

03Information collected automatically

Analytics (only if you click Accept). If you accept cookies in the banner, our site loads an analytics tool from IdentityPixel. It records how the site is used: pages viewed, the page that referred you, clicks, scrolling, time on page, and basic device and browser details such as screen size, language and time zone. It stores a visitor ID in your browser’s local storage, and its servers receive your IP address. The provider may use this information to help identify the organization or person associated with a visit. If you decline, this tool is never loaded.

Your cookie choice. We store your Accept or Decline choice in your browser so we don’t ask again on every page.

Technical logs. Like any website, our hosting provider automatically records basic technical information such as IP address, browser type and the time of each request, to keep the site running and secure.

Fonts. Our pages load typefaces from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google’s servers, which see your IP address.

04How we use your information

We use your information to respond to your enquiry, book and carry out site visits, prepare quotes, manage projects you hire us for, and keep business records we are required to keep.

We use analytics information, where you have accepted it, to understand how visitors use the site and improve it.

We do not sell your personal information.

05Who we share it with

We share personal information only with service providers that help us run the site and our business, and only as needed for that purpose:

  • Hostinger, which hosts our website and processes form submissions.
  • FormSubmit, a backup service that delivers form submissions to our inbox if our main form delivery fails.
  • Our email provider, which receives and stores messages sent to us.
  • IdentityPixel, our analytics provider, only if you accept cookies.
  • Google, which serves the fonts used on our pages.

We may also disclose information where the law requires or permits it.

06Storage outside Canada

Some of these service providers may store or process personal information outside Canada, including in the United States. When that happens, the information is subject to the laws of that country, and authorities there may be able to access it. You can contact us with questions about how our service providers outside Canada handle personal information.

07Your choices

You can accept or decline analytics in the cookie banner, and change your mind at any time using the “Cookie settings” link at the bottom of every page. You can also clear your browser’s stored site data.

You don’t have to use our online form. You can call us at 587.834.4444 instead.

08How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as we need them to respond and follow up. If you become a client, we keep project records for as long as needed for the project, warranty and legal or tax purposes. After that, we delete or destroy it securely.

09How we protect it

We use reasonable safeguards to protect personal information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) on our site and limited access to the records we keep. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect the information you share with us.

10Access and correction

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask questions about this policy. Contact us using the details below and we will respond within the time required by law.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner of Alberta.

11Children

Our services are for businesses, and our site is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

12Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
